Privacy Policy

Last updated: {{ updated }}

What to Watch ("the Service") is a personal watchlist app for movies, series and anime, run by an individual developer in Italy. This policy explains what the Service collects, why, and what you can do about it. It is short because the Service collects little.

What we collect

  • Google account basics. When you sign in with Google we receive your name, email address and profile picture URL. We do not request access to your Gmail, Drive, Contacts or any other Google data.
  • Your profile. The username you choose and the avatar shown to friends.
  • Your watchlist. The titles you add, their status, ratings, season and episode progress, and reactions.
  • Your friends. The invite links you create and the connections you accept. Connected friends can see each other's watchlists.
  • Chat messages. If you use the recommendation chat, the messages you send are forwarded to an AI provider to generate a reply.
  • Technical logs. Our hosting and database providers keep standard server logs (IP address, timestamp, requested URL) for security and troubleshooting. We do not run analytics, advertising or tracking cookies.

Why we use it

Only to run the Service: to sign you in, store your watchlist, show it to the friends you have chosen, answer your chat requests, and keep the Service working and secure. We do not sell your data, use it for advertising, or send you marketing email.

Who else processes it

  • Google — sign-in.
  • Supabase — authentication, database and server functions, where your account and watchlist are stored.
  • Vercel — hosting of the web app.
  • MiMo — the AI model that answers chat messages. It receives the conversation text, not your account details.
  • The Movie Database (TMDB) — film and series metadata, posters and images. Your browser loads images from TMDB directly, so TMDB sees your IP address. This product uses the TMDB API but is not endorsed or certified by TMDB.

These providers act on our behalf and may store data outside Italy, including in the United States, under the safeguards their own terms provide. Nobody else receives your data unless the law requires it.

Google user data

The Service's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Your Google name, email and profile picture are used solely to create and identify your account and are never sold or used for advertising.

How long we keep it

Your account and watchlist are kept while your account exists. Delete your account and the associated data is removed; server logs and encrypted backups may retain copies for a short period before they rotate out. See how to delete your account; to ask for a copy or correction of your data, email us at the address below and we will action it.

Security

Data is stored on Supabase with row-level security, so a signed-in user can reach their own rows and those their friends have shared, and nothing else. No system is perfectly secure, but we use the protections our providers offer and keep secrets on the server.

Children

The Service is not directed at anyone under 16, and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will remove it.

Your rights

If you are in the EEA or the UK, you have the right to access, correct, export, delete or restrict the processing of your personal data, and to complain to your local data protection authority. We rely on your consent and on the necessity of processing to provide the Service you asked for. Email us to exercise any of these rights.

Changes

If this policy changes we will update this page and the date at the top. Significant changes will be announced in the app before they take effect.

Contact

Questions, requests or deletions: simonerizzo.pvt@gmail.com

Delete your What to Watch account

Last updated: {{ updated }}

This page explains how to delete your account, and the data associated with it, in What to Watch, the app published by Simone Rizzo. You can do it yourself from inside the app, or ask us by email. Either way it is free, permanent and cannot be undone.

How to delete your account in the app

  1. Open What to Watch and sign in.
  2. Tap the account icon — top right on phones, the bottom of the sidebar on a wide screen.
  3. Choose Delete account.
  4. Type DELETE to confirm, then tap Delete my account. The deletion happens immediately and cannot be undone.

How to request deletion by email

If you cannot reach the app — you lost access to the device or the account — write to us.

  1. Send an email to simonerizzo.pvt@gmail.com from the same Google address you use to sign in — that is how we verify the request is yours.
  2. Use the subject line “Delete my What to Watch account”.
  3. Include the username shown on your profile, so we can match the request if you have more than one account.
  4. We reply to confirm within 7 days, and the account is deleted within 30 days of that confirmation. You do not need to do anything else; uninstalling the app on its own does not delete your data.

What is deleted

Everything tied to your account is erased from our database:

  • Account and profile — the name, email address and profile picture URL received from Google when you sign in, your chosen username and avatar.
  • Watchlist — every title you added, with its status, rating, season and episode progress, and reactions.
  • Friends — your connections with other users and any invite links you created. Friends stop seeing your watchlist.

Chat messages sent to the recommendation assistant are not stored on our side at all: they are forwarded to the AI provider to produce a reply and kept only in your browser session.

What is kept, and for how long

  • Encrypted database backups — up to 30 days after deletion, then overwritten as backups rotate. Your data is not restored from them except to recover the whole database from an incident.
  • Server logs (IP address, timestamp, requested URL) held by our hosting and database providers for security and troubleshooting — up to 30 days, then discarded automatically.
  • The deletion request email itself, kept as the record that you asked and we acted, for up to 12 months.

Nothing is kept for advertising, profiling or resale. If a law requires us to retain something longer, we will say so in our reply.

Deleting only some data

You do not have to delete the whole account: you can remove individual titles from your watchlist in the app at any time, and you can ask us by email to remove your friend connections or reset your username while keeping the account.
